# Independent product observation protocol

Status: prepared; no independent sessions have been conducted by this engineering run.

Audience: solo builders using coding agents who can bring a real completion message and a repository they are authorized to inspect. Initial qualitative target: five to eight participants. This is a planning target, not statistical validation.

Do not send invitations or contact anyone without the owner's explicit authorization. Never publish private source, credentials or participant identities.

1. Record an anonymous session ID, date, source revision and the participant's experience level. Obtain consent to record observations.
2. Ask the participant to review an actual agent handoff with their usual method. Record time, decision and uncertainties without supplying the expected answer.
3. Ask them to inspect the same handoff in VibeCheck. Record whether they can start, find the key evidence, explain the verdict and identify its limits without coaching.
4. Record usefulness and errors: a new issue found, an incorrect finding, missing evidence, confusion, abandonment or no benefit. Do not count every UNVERIFIED label as a caught mistake.
5. If they make an actual correction, record the approved public revision and recheck the same obligation. A changed model answer without a relevant source change does not establish improvement.
6. Where a task comparison is feasible, vary task order and retain both timing records. Repeated exposure and coaching can bias apparent time savings; report those limitations.
